What TEKRISE costs

Last updated: 7 September 2026 By: the TEKRISE founder
THE SHORT ANSWER

TEKRISE is free to start: full AI swing analyses, permanently, funded by a short ad, with no credit card and no account required. Paid plans raise that limit and remove ads — Plus $14.90, Pro $29.90 and Ultra $69.90 per month in USD, with annual plans available at a discount.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Free is permanent, not a trial. Full analyses, ad-funded, no card, no account.
  • Paid plans change only two things: the daily limit and the ads. No features are locked away.
  • Plus $14.90 / Pro $29.90 / Ultra $69.90 per month; annual available at a discount.
  • New Pro subscribers get 50% off the first 3 months.
  • Billing and cancellation are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

02 What you get for free

The free plan is not a trial that expires. It gives you full AI swing analyses, permanently, funded by a short ad that plays while your swing is being analysed. There is no credit card and no account required to start — you can install the app and analyse a swing immediately.

Free analyses are not a cut-down version. You get the same pose tracking, the same ranked technique findings, the same drill plan and the same progress tracking as a paid plan. The only differences are how many you can run and whether an ad plays.

03 Which plan do you actually need

  • Free suits most players. You are meant to work on one fault at a time, not analyse thirty swings — a handful of analyses goes further than you would think.
  • Plus makes sense if you play several times a week, want to compare a few swings per session, and would rather not watch ads.
  • Pro suits players training seriously most days, or a coach reviewing a handful of students.
  • Ultra is built for coaches and clubs running analyses across a full roster.
Start free

There is no reason to pay before you know the app works for your swing and your filming setup. Run a few free analyses first; the paid tiers only raise the daily limit and remove ads.

04 Why there is a paid tier at all

Every analysis costs us money — server time and AI inference on every single swing. The free tier is funded by ads and by subscribers. That is the whole model: no data selling, no outside investors.

05 Cancelling and managing your plan

  • Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so cancel from your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Cancelling stops the renewal; you keep the paid plan until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Switching between monthly and annual, or between tiers, is handled by the store.
  • Deleting your TEKRISE account is separate from cancelling a subscription — see deleting your account.

Frequently asked

Is TEKRISE free?
Yes. The free plan gives you full AI swing analyses, permanently, funded by a short ad while your swing is analysed. No credit card and no account are required to start. Free analyses are the full product, not a cut-down version — paid plans only raise the limit and remove ads.
How much does TEKRISE cost?
Plus is $14.90 a month for 10 analyses a day, Pro is $29.90 for 30 a day, and Ultra is $69.90 for 200 a day, all in USD. Annual plans are available at a discount, and new Pro subscribers get 50% off the first 3 months.
What is the difference between the paid plans?
Only the number of AI swing analyses per day: 10 on Plus, 30 on Pro, 200 on Ultra. All paid plans remove ads and include the same analysis, drill plans, progress tracking and coach tools. There are no features locked behind the higher tiers.
How do I cancel my TEKRISE subscription?
Through your App Store or Google Play account settings, since Apple and Google handle the billing rather than us. Cancelling stops the renewal and you keep the paid plan until the end of the period you have already paid for.
Do I need an account to use TEKRISE?
No. You can start analysing swings without creating an account or entering a credit card. An account is only needed if you want your history to follow you to another device, or to use the coach features.
