4-STEP PROCESS

HOW IT
WORKS

01
Record your swing ON YOUR PHONE
Prop up your phone or ask a friend. Any angle works — side-on gives the most data. 15–60 seconds of footage is plenty.
02
AI tracks your body frame by frame ON-DEVICE · INSTANT
Pose tracking runs on your phone — ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS — following 12 body joints frame by frame. On Android, racquet and ball detection runs on-device alongside it. Your full recording never leaves the device.
03
You get a full coaching report IMPROVEMENTS RANKED
Findings ranked by severity. Personalised drill plan. Progress tracked across every session.

WHO'S BEHIND TEKRISE

Made by players.

TEKRISE is a small, independent project built by tennis enthusiasts — people who love the game, got tired of plateauing, and wanted honest feedback that didn't cost a fortune. We're not a big company and we don't have outside investors. That's exactly why your support matters: it keeps us independent, and it keeps the free tier open.

I built TEKRISE because the feedback that actually changes your technique — someone watching closely and telling you what your body just did — is expensive, occasional, and unavailable to most players. FREQUENTLY ASKED

Got questions?
We've got answers.

Will TEKRISE replace my tennis coach?
No — and it's not meant to. TEKRISE does one thing: it measures your swing and shows you what's happening. That's maybe 10–15% of what a great coach does — it can't read your opponent, run live drills, feed balls, fix your grip mid-swing, or keep you motivated. Think of it as the slow-motion replay and the measuring tape: the eye, not the coach.
I don't have a coach — is this still for me?
Absolutely. If you practise on your own, TEKRISE gives you the clear, technical feedback that's otherwise hard to get without someone watching — so you work on the right thing next instead of grooving the same mistake. Improve on your own schedule, at your own pace.
I'm a coach — how does TEKRISE help me?
It solves your biggest problem: students reinforcing bad habits in the six days between lessons. They arrive having practised the right things, so you spend less time repeating corrections and more on strategy, footwork and match play. A Coach dashboard shows what each student worked on, and you earn a share on every student you refer. See For Coaches →
Is the app really free?
It's free to start — no credit card, no account required — and there will always be a free tier. You get AI swing analysis on the free plan, funded by a short ad while your swing is being analysed. Running it isn't free for us (servers and AI cost money on every analysis), so there are optional paid subscriptions that lift the daily limit and remove the ads.
Where does my donation go?
Straight into keeping TEKRISE alive: the server and AI costs behind every analysis, new features and shot types, and the time it takes to keep improving the app. Donations help keep the free tier as generous as we can make it. We're an independent project with no outside investors.
How accurate is the AI?
TEKRISE tracks your body pose frame-by-frame on-device and checks your racket using AI vision around the moment of impact. It's genuinely useful for spotting technique patterns and tracking change over time — but it's an estimate from a single phone camera, not a motion-capture lab. We're honest about that: it's a guide, not gospel, but it will keep getting better.
How do I support TEKRISE?
The easiest way is a one-off donation via Ko-fi — give whatever feels right, even a couple of dollars helps. You can also help by telling a hitting partner about the app, or sending us feedback so we can make it better. Questions? Email hello@tekrise.app.
