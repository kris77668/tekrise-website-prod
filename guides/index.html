Tennis technique guides

Last updated: 7 September 2026 By: the TEKRISE founder
THE SHORT ANSWER

Guides to tennis technique built around measurements rather than opinions — what each swing fault is, the healthy range for it, how to film your own swing usefully, and what phone-based analysis can and cannot tell you.

Where to start

If you are not sure which guide you need:

  • You want to know what is wrong with your swing — start with swing faults, measured. It lists every fault with its healthy range, so you can check your own footage against real numbers.
  • You want your forehand to improve specifically — how to improve your forehand covers the five things that matter and the order to do them in.
  • You are wondering whether any of this actually works — AI swing analysis, explained is the honest version, including what it cannot do.
  • You train without a coach — how to practise tennis alone is written for you.

Tennis swing faults, measured

Every technique fault TEKRISE detects — what each one is, the exact measurement and healthy range, and how to fix it. Includes which fault to fix first.

How to improve your tennis forehand

The five changes that actually improve a club-level forehand, the order to fix them in, and how to film your swing so the footage is worth analysing.

AI tennis swing analysis, explained

What happens to your video, what phone-based analysis measures reliably, what it cannot measure, and an honest comparison with a human coach.

How to practise tennis alone

Why solo practice often produces no improvement, how to structure a session that does, and how to track progress with nobody watching.

Why we publish this

TEKRISE measures tennis swings from phone video. Publishing the measurements, the healthy ranges and the limits is deliberate: a number you cannot interrogate is not worth much. If your arm is at 138° at contact, you should be able to look up what that means, what good looks like, and how confident the measurement is.

Everything in these guides applies whether or not you use the app. The measurements are just tennis.
