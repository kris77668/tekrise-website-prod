AI tennis swing analysis, explained

Last updated: 7 September 2026 By: the TEKRISE founder
THE SHORT ANSWER

AI swing analysis tracks your body joints frame by frame from a phone video, converts them into coach-readable measurements like joint angles and shoulder rotation, and compares them against healthy ranges. It is accurate enough to spot technique patterns and track change over time, but it is a 2-D estimate from one camera — not a motion-capture lab, and not a coach.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Pose tracking runs on your phone. Only a short silent clip is sent for the written coaching feedback.
  • Reliable for: relative change over time, large deviations, consistency between swings, and timing within a clip.
  • Unreliable for: absolute precision, depth, grip, spin rate, and any measurement from badly off-axis footage.
  • Camera angle matters more than camera quality. It is the single largest factor in analysis quality.
  • It is a guide, not gospel — and roughly a tenth of what a human coach does.

01 What actually happens to your video

The phrase "AI swing analysis" covers several quite different things. Here is the specific pipeline TEKRISE uses, because "AI" on its own tells you nothing about accuracy.

  • Pose tracking, on your phone. A pose model finds 12 body joints — shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles — in every frame. This runs on the device itself: ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS. Nothing is uploaded for this step.
  • Racquet and ball detection, also on your phone (Android). A separate object-detection model locates the racquet and ball. On iPhone this model is not shipped yet, so the analysis there runs on pose alone.
  • Derived measurements. Raw joint coordinates are useless to a coach. They are converted into readable quantities: joint angles, hip–shoulder separation, torso lean, stance width relative to your own body, racquet-head speed, follow-through ratio.
  • Coaching feedback. A short silent clip — at most 3 seconds, the swing you chose — is sent to our AI coaching engine, which returns the written coaching. Your full original recording stays on your phone.

02 How accurate is it, honestly

Accurate enough to spot technique patterns and track change over time. Not accurate enough to be treated as a laboratory measurement. That distinction is the whole answer, and it is worth being precise about why.

What it is genuinely good at

  • Relative change. Comparing your shoulder turn this month against last month, filmed the same way, is reliable — systematic errors mostly cancel.
  • Large, obvious deviations. An elbow at 130° when it should be near 165° is far outside the noise floor.
  • Consistency. Whether you do the same thing every swing, which is often more diagnostic than any single value.
  • Timing. When events happen relative to each other — coil peak versus contact — because that is measured within one clip.

What it is not good at

  • Absolute precision. A single degree of difference between two swings is noise, not signal.
  • Depth. One camera sees two dimensions. Anything happening towards or away from the lens is inferred, not measured.
  • Anything off-axis. Widths and stances are projections and compress as the camera moves off-square. TEKRISE classifies your camera view and skips the checks that stop being trustworthy rather than reporting a confident wrong number.
  • Grip, string tension, spin rate, ball speed. None of these are visible to a pose model.
  • Whether the shot was the right choice. Technique and tactics are different questions.
The honest summary

Treat it as a very patient training partner with an excellent memory and a slow-motion replay — not as a measuring instrument, and not as a coach. It is a guide, not gospel.

04 How this compares with a human coach

It is not a competition, and it is worth being clear about the size of the gap. Measuring a swing is perhaps a tenth of what a good coach does.

05 What happens to your video

  • Pose tracking — and racquet detection on Android — happens on your device. Your footage is not uploaded for those steps.
  • Only a short silent clip is sent for coaching feedback — at most 3 seconds, the swing you selected, with no audio track. That clip is also stored with your analysis history so you can replay it later; you can have it deleted with your account.
  • Your full original recording stays on your phone unless you choose to delete it.

Frequently asked

How accurate is AI tennis swing analysis?
Accurate enough to identify technique patterns and track change over time; not accurate enough to be a laboratory measurement. It is reliable for large deviations, consistency between swings, and timing within a clip. It is unreliable for absolute precision, anything happening towards or away from the camera, and any measurement taken from badly off-axis footage.
Does AI swing analysis work from a normal phone camera?
Yes. Pose tracking runs on the phone itself using standard on-device models. Camera angle and framing matter far more than camera quality — a square-on or side-on shot with your whole body including your feet in frame beats a higher-resolution clip filmed from a bad angle.
Can AI replace a tennis coach?
No, and the gap is large. Measuring a swing is perhaps a tenth of what a coach does. Software cannot feed you balls, correct your grip mid-rally, teach tactics, read your opponent, or judge when you need pushing rather than encouragement. What it does well is cover the days between lessons, when most players practise unsupervised.
Does my tennis video get uploaded to the cloud?
Pose and racquet detection run entirely on your device. Only a short silent clip — at most 3 seconds, the swing you chose — is sent for the written coaching feedback. Your full original recording stays on your phone.
Why does the camera angle matter so much?
A single camera sees two dimensions, so any measurement involving width or depth is a projection that compresses as the camera moves off-square. Rather than report a confident wrong number, TEKRISE classifies your camera view and skips the measurements that stop being trustworthy at that angle.
